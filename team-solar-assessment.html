Team Rooftop Solar Assessment

Use this page after a customer has shown interest and shared their property location. Enter the lead details and exact address to retrieve Google's available rooftop solar model data before arranging the physical site assessment.

1. Lead & property details

Only use this tool for a customer who has requested a solar consultation and provided the property location for assessment. The address is sent to Google Geocoding and Solar APIs. Do not enter bank details, ID numbers or other sensitive personal data.

2. Assessment result

A successful Google lookup will show the modelled panel capacity, panel count, panel-array area and solar indicators here. Some buildings may not have available imagery.
Important: Google data is only a preliminary model. Our team must verify actual roof access, shade, roof structure, electrical load, DISCOM requirements and site conditions before recommending a system or issuing a quotation.

Authorised Kashi Solar & Renewables staff only. Access code is required for every assessment and is not saved by this page.
