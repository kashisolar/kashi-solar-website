Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Kashi Solar & Renewables (“we”, “our” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information you provide when you contact us or request information, a quotation, or a solar estimate through our website, Google Ads lead forms, telephone, or WhatsApp.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you contact us, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, city or village, property type, monthly electricity-bill range, preferred solar system size, and details you choose to include in your enquiry.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, prepare a solar estimate or quotation, explain relevant solar installation and subsidy processes, discuss financing options you ask about, arrange follow-up or site assessment, and improve our customer service. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing of Information

We may use service providers that help operate our website, forms, communications, or advertising, and may share information when necessary to respond to your request, provide a service you have requested, comply with applicable law, or protect our rights. We do not authorize service providers to use your information for unrelated purposes.

Advertising and Lead Forms

If you submit a lead form through Google Ads, the information you choose to submit may be shared with us by Google so that we can respond to your enquiry. Google may process information according to its own privacy policies and settings. Please review Google's privacy information for details about its services.

Data Retention and Security

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including customer follow-up, business records, and legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your Choices

You may ask us to update or delete your enquiry information, subject to applicable record-keeping or legal requirements. You can contact us using the details below. You may also choose not to provide requested information, though we may then be unable to prepare an estimate or respond fully.

Children's Privacy

Our solar enquiry services are intended for adults and businesses. Please do not submit a child's personal information through our forms.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version and its update date will be published on this page.
