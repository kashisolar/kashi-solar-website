How do I know what system size I need?

System sizing depends on your electricity use, available shadow-free roof area, connection and site conditions. Share your bill range and our team can guide you through the assessment.

Can I get a government subsidy?

Eligible residential consumers may qualify under the applicable PM Surya Ghar rules. Eligibility, approved capacity, DISCOM process and current policy determine the amount. Commercial and industrial projects follow different arrangements.

Do you help with bank loans?

We can guide you on enquiries and documentation for participating banks. The bank independently decides approval, interest rate, tenure and terms.

How long does installation take?

It depends on site readiness, system size, approvals, equipment and weather. After assessment, our team can share a project-specific timeline.

Which areas do you serve?

Our core service area includes Raebareli, Lucknow, Amethi, Unnao, Fatehpur, Pratapgarh, Kanpur and nearby districts in Uttar Pradesh. Contact us to confirm your location.

Will I receive a price immediately?

We prepare a customised quotation after understanding your requirements and site conditions. We do not publish a one-price-fits-all system rate.
